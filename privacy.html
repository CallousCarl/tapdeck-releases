TapDeck Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

TapDeck turns a touch screen into a deck of buttons for your PC. It runs entirely on your computer. We don't collect, store or sell any personal information, and TapDeck has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your PC

Your profiles, pages, keys, icons and settings are saved in a folder on your PC (%APPDATA%\TapDeck). They are never uploaded. Uninstalling TapDeck and deleting that folder removes them.

TapDeck runs a small web server that only your own PC can reach (address 127.0.0.1). Its editor and deck windows use it; other computers on your network can't connect to it.

When TapDeck uses the internet

  • Website icons. When you add a key that opens a website, TapDeck downloads that site's icon from the site itself, so the key can show it. The website sees an ordinary request from your PC, as it would if you visited it.
  • Weather widgets. When you search for a place for a weather widget, the words you type are sent to OpenStreetMap's place search (Nominatim). While a weather widget is on the deck, TapDeck asks MET Norway for that place's forecast, sending only the place's coordinates rounded to about a kilometre, at most once every half hour. Nothing else about you or your PC is sent. Without weather widgets, neither service is contacted.
  • Keys you set up. Keys that open websites or apps do exactly what you set them to do.

The Microsoft Store edition doesn't check for updates itself; the Microsoft Store handles updates.

Other apps on your PC

If you use them, TapDeck talks to OBS Studio and LibreHardwareMonitor on your own PC to control recording and to show temperatures. It reads which app is in front so the deck can switch to that app's profile and send keys to it. None of this leaves your PC.

Children

TapDeck doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
